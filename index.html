There’s something I want you to know. 🫂

01 · Before you

Ik it's your day, and I don't want to make you feel bad, but I just want to tell you something. I've had the worst friendships throughout my school life until I met you. And I think it's important to tell you about my past. 🥹❤️

I was always bullied in school because of my weight, face, skin tone, and body physique. I barely had any friends, and the ones I had were also so-called friends. Then I went from 5th to 10th grade at BNPS, and I swear, my school life was so horrible. I never wanted to experience that same kind of atmosphere or have the same type of classmates again.

02 · The plot twist 😭

Then came 11th grade, and guess what? We were in the SAME CLASS, but we never talked. 😭😭 I didn't even know you existed, and hamari toh kabhi bani hi nahi thi. 😂😭 I was also a little rude towards your group back then. Like, imagine, we were in the same class, and look at us now. 😭🫶🏻

But finally, the wait was over... 12th grade! OH MY GOD, FUCKING BEST DAYS EVER. 😭❤️

Maine apna phone Tara ko diya tha ki tu Vijay ko call kar le. Ik, vo bkl hai 😭😂. I don't want to remind you of that person, but somehow, that's how we slowly started talking a little more.

And then came the moment when I realised ki haan bhai, is ladki se meri friendship ho sakti hai. 😭🫶🏻

Meri pehle bhi Tani aur Pretti se utni baat nahi hoti thi. Somehow, I don't hate them, but I don't feel that much closeness with them either.

03 · The moment I knew 🥹

Now, let me tell you the moment that made me realise how special you were. It was the Beauty and Wellness practical day, when Shrestha was doing my makeup. You, Tani, and Pretti came and saw me, and you guys started fucking shouting for me. 😭😭❤️

And I was literally like, BAHIII, WHATTTT?! Itna toh kabhi kisi ne mere liye nahi kiya tha. 🥹💗 That little moment made me realise that female friendships aren't always toxic. You CAN trust people, and not everyone is going to hurt you.

04 · What you changed ❤️

And that's something I'll always be grateful to you for, sweetheart. Thank you for making me believe that genuine friendships exist, that girls can genuinely love and support each other, and that I deserve a friendship where I feel wanted, loved, and accepted. 🫂❤️

I LOVE YOU FUCKING SO MUCH, SWEETHEARTTTT. 😭🎀❤️🧿
